# SLS — Shamase Logistics Solutions 🚛

![SLS Logo](src/assets/images/sls-logo.png)

## Moving Goods. Building Connections.

**Shamase Logistics Solutions (SLS)** is a South African road transportation and logistics business focused on providing reliable, professional, and flexible transport solutions for businesses.

This project is the official web presence being developed for SLS, with the website designed to support the company's current operations while remaining scalable as the fleet, services, and customer base grow.

---

## 🌍 About SLS

Shamase Logistics Solutions is a growing transport business currently operating with a small fleet while working toward expanding its transportation capabilities.

The current fleet includes:

- 🚛 FAW 8D Tipper Truck
- 🚚 FAW 8-Ton Truck

SLS is focused on developing long-term relationships with businesses and pursuing recurring and contract-based transportation opportunities.

The website therefore focuses not only on individual transport enquiries, but also on presenting SLS as a professional and scalable business-to-business transportation partner.

---

## 🎯 Project Vision

The goal of this project is to create a professional digital presence for SLS that communicates:

- Reliability
- Professionalism
- Flexible transport solutions
- Business-to-business services
- Long-term partnerships
- Contract transportation
- Fleet growth
- Customer-focused communication

The website is designed with future expansion in mind so that additional vehicles, services, industries, and capabilities can be incorporated without requiring a complete redesign.

---

## 🚛 Transport Solutions

The website currently presents the following core transport solutions:

### General Freight

Road transportation for suitable commercial goods, materials, equipment, and supplies.

### Tipper Transport

Bulk material transportation suitable for construction, infrastructure, and related applications.

### Dedicated Transport

Transport capacity for businesses with recurring or ongoing transportation requirements.

### Contract Transport

Long-term transport arrangements designed around the operational requirements of businesses requiring dependable vehicle capacity.

## ✨ Website Features

### 🏠 Professional Homepage

The homepage introduces SLS through a modern logistics-focused design with:

- Hero section
- Company introduction
- Transport solutions
- Fleet showcase
- Industries served
- Why SLS section
- Contract transport call-to-action
- Business enquiry section

---

### 🚛 Fleet Showcase

The website provides a dedicated section for presenting the SLS fleet.

Current vehicles:

- FAW 8D Tipper Truck
- FAW 8-Ton Truck

The fleet structure is designed to support the addition of future vehicles.

---

### 📦 Transport Solutions

Visitors can explore the different transportation services offered by SLS and identify the solution that best matches their requirements.

---

### 🤝 Contract Transport

A dedicated call-to-action encourages businesses requiring recurring transportation capacity to contact SLS regarding potential long-term transport arrangements.

---

### 💬 WhatsApp Enquiries

The website integrates WhatsApp as a primary communication channel.

Visitors can initiate enquiries directly through WhatsApp for:

- Transport quotations
- Contract transport discussions
- General enquiries
- Business communication

The WhatsApp number is centrally configurable so that business contact information can be updated without modifying multiple components.

---

### 📱 Responsive Design

The website is designed to work across:

- Desktop computers
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Android devices
- iPhones

The mobile experience is particularly important because customers can contact SLS directly through WhatsApp.
