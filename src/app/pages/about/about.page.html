About SLS

Building a Transport Business for the Road Ahead

Shamase Logistics Solutions (SLS) is a new South African road transport business, currently operating with a FAW 8D tipper truck and a FAW 8-ton truck. SLS is focused on building a reliable transport operation from the ground up, with professional service and clear communication at the centre of how the business works.

Rather than relying only on once-off loads, SLS is built around developing long-term relationships with business customers — through dedicated transport capacity and contract transport arrangements designed around a client's operational requirements.

As SLS grows, the fleet and range of transport solutions will grow with it. The goal is straightforward: dependable road transportation, delivered by a business that customers can build a long-term working relationship with.
